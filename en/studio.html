Studio · Bonomelli Estate

Projects born from stone, glass and garden, for those who build homes meant to last.

Bonomelli Estate is the visual concept studio of Brenda Bonomelli, based in San Bonifacio, near Verona. It designs pavilions, greenhouses and stone furniture for the luxury residential market, and develops them with craftsmen, architects and manufacturers.

Every concept can be developed to measure or offered as a private commission.

Brenda Bonomelli · Visual Concept Designer

From drawing to stone

The method

Every pavilion begins as a concept and becomes built architecture through four stages, followed by the studio from start to finish.

  1. I

    The concept

    We study the place and the house. Renders and films of the pavilion follow, shaped to the park, the view and the use you have in mind.

  2. II

    The development

    With licensed architects and partner craftsmen, the concept becomes a technical project: dimensions, structure, foundations, permits.

  3. III

    The materials

    Stone is chosen, travertine or Sardinian breccia, with hand-forged iron and glazing. A detailed quote follows.

  4. IV

    The installation

    The modular system is produced, shipped and assembled on site by our partners, overseen by the studio.
