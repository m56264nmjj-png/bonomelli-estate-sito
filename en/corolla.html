COROLLA

An armchair that opens like a flower. The forest-green velvet back is divided into soft petals that close around the seat; stone holds it, and a fine brass line marks where fabric meets stone. One design, in three versions.

The seat rests on a stone ring, like a flower on its vase.

Forest-green velvet, petal back
