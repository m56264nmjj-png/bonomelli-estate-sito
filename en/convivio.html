Chiampo travertine

The top is a slab of travertine worked by Industria Marmi Ferrari in Chiampo, at the heart of the Veneto stone district. Travertine is a warm, luminous stone, with small pores and veining that make every top different.

The hand-forged iron base

The base is a blacksmith’s work: two crossed arches, a forged rosette, a stretcher with a central knot and a wavy band that holds the stone. Hand-forged iron keeps the marks of its making, which set it apart from industrial metalwork.

Frequently asked questions

Can CONVIVIO be made to measure?

The standard size is 100 × 50 × h 45 cm. Other sizes can be requested: Brenda checks them with the craftsmen and replies with a quote.

Why is there glass over the travertine?

Travertine is a porous stone: the tempered glass protects it from stains and spills and makes the top smooth and easy to clean, while the stone stays visible.

How do I clean it?

Clean the glass like any household glass. For the stone edges a soft, slightly damp cloth is enough; avoid acidic or harsh products, which damage travertine.

Can it be used outdoors?

It is designed for interiors and covered spaces such as loggias and porticoes. For open-air use, the iron finish must be discussed with the craftsmen before ordering.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

CONVIVIO costs €1,290 and arrives within 2 weeks of the order. Shipping and packing are quoted separately, based on the delivery address.
