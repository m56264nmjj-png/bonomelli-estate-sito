A bespoke iron and glass greenhouse

The Dream Greenhouse is an arched iron and glass garden gallery, somewhere between a Victorian greenhouse, an orangery and a winter garden. Burnished iron arches trace a covered path through the garden, the glass panels carry floral motifs and the Via Florae pebble mosaic runs underfoot. It is an original design by Brenda Bonomelli, registered in the European Union (EUIPO no. 015156041).

Four flowers: wisteria, lily, hydrangea, sunflower

The greenhouse comes in four versions, each devoted to a flower that returns in the glass and in the floor mosaic. Every commission is adapted to the garden: the length of the gallery, the number of arches, attached to the house or freestanding in the grounds.

How a commission comes to life

1. The concept: renderings and video of the greenhouse in its garden. 2. Development with licensed architects and partner craftsmen, including working drawings, checks and permits. 3. The choice of materials and a detailed quote. 4. Production and installation, supervised by the studio. The images on this page are visual concepts.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a greenhouse, a lemon house and an orangery?

A greenhouse is mainly for plants and has a lot of glass. A lemon house shelters citrus trees in winter. An orangery is its elegant version, with masonry and tall windows, also used as a living room. The Dream Greenhouse joins the light of a greenhouse with the elegance of an orangery.

Do I need a permit for an iron and glass greenhouse?

It depends on the municipality, the size and any landscape or heritage restrictions on the property. A permanent greenhouse usually needs a building permit under Italian rules: a licensed professional checks this before production. Bonomelli Estate works with licensed architects who handle this step.

How much does a bespoke iron and glass greenhouse cost?

It depends on the length of the gallery, the number of arches, the type of glass and the site. After the concept, the studio prepares a detailed quote.

What glass is used?

The type and thickness of the glass are chosen in the working drawings with the craftsmen, based on the climate, the use of the greenhouse and safety rules.

Can it be used as a winter garden or for events?

Yes: the glass gallery is designed to be lived in, as a covered walk among flowers, a winter garden or a place for dinners and receptions.

Can the Dream Greenhouse be copied?

No: it is a registered EU design (EUIPO no. 015156041). It is made only by commission from Bonomelli Estate.
