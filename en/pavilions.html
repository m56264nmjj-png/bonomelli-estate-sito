The Pavilions

Small temples, rotundas and orangeries in natural stone, born of classical proportion and made for the parks of great estates. Each one stands on its own: a library, a summer salon, a room for receptions.

BONOMELLI Two octagonal storeys, a library and a spiral stair. CAELO Three glass domes, receptions and private dinners. MAGNOLIA A three-dome hemicycle over the parterre. ROME A paired-column temple on the water. D’HIER A glazed Ionic rotunda among the hedges. FORESTIER The woodland pavilion. AIR FRAIS A colonnaded rotunda among hydrangeas and lavender.

Construction · common to every pavilion

System
Modular, in natural stone
Stones
Travertine or Sardinian breccia
Metalwork
Hand-forged iron
Glazing
Triple-layer tempered glass
Energy · optional
Flexible thin-film solar panels with magnetic mounting. Supplied separately, on the manufacturer's quote.
