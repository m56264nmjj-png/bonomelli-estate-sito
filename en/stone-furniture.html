CONVIVIO

A low table in hand-forged iron and travertine. The base is made of two crossed arches joined by a forged rosette and a stretcher with a central knot; a wavy iron band holds the travertine slab, protected by tempered glass. The piece exists and is made to order exactly as shown, together with three Veneto firms that partner with the studio.
