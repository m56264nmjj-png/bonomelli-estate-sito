How I work and what it costs

First the idea in your place, then the stone. Every step has a clear price.

Every project begins with a concept, paid separately: renderings and a film of the piece in your own garden, villa or hotel. Then, if you wish to build it, Bonomelli Estate also oversees the construction with a partner company: technical design, permits, production and installation, with a detailed quote and one point of contact from the idea to the finished piece.

The prices below are indicative, “from”, excluding VAT. The final price depends on size, stone, site and access.

From concept to installation

How it works

One point of contact from the idea to the finished garden.

  1. I

    The concept

    From photos and measurements, or a site visit, come renderings and a film of the piece in its setting.

  2. II

    The quote

    Stone, iron and glass are chosen with our partner company and craftsmen, followed by a detailed quote with timings.

  3. III

    Design and permits

    Licensed architects prepare the construction design, the structural checks and the permit applications.

  4. IV

    Production and installation

    Stone, iron and glass are produced, shipped and assembled on site, overseen by the studio.
